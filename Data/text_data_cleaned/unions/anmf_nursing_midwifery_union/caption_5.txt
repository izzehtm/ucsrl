#NAIDOCWeek is celebrating 50 Years of Deadly. 

For 50 years, we have been coming together at NAIDOC events that champion truth-telling, justice and self-determination for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples.

NAIDOC Week has hundreds of events happening around the country. An opportunity to learn more about our shared histories, join with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people, and listen and grow connections with First Nations deep knowledge, culture, and excellence.
