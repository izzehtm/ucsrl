The country’s largest union, the ANMF, has paid tribute to the work of ACTU Secretary Sally McManus and President Michele O’Neil, who have announced they are leaving the organisation.

ANMF Federal Secretary Annie Butler, who also sits as ACTU Vice President, said Sally and Michele made an extraordinary collective contribution to the nation’s industrial relations (IR) system, delivering a raft of reforms that improved the everyday lives of Australian workers.
