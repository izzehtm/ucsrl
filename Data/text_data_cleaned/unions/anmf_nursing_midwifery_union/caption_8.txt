Nurses and midwives are the solution. 

At Federal Parliament, ANMF Federal Secretary Annie Butler and Federal Assistant Secretary Catelyn Richards joined nursing and midwifery organisations, showing parliamentarians first-hand what nurses and midwives can deliver in the primary care sector with innovative, evidence-based, person-centred nurse- and midwife-led solutions. 

From nurse-led skin-cancer checks and wound closure to rural and remote-area advanced care, and maternity care for both mothers and babies, the Parliamentary Friends of Nursing event in the Mural Hall, highlights the growing role of nurses and midwives in delivering timely, effective health care across the community, enabling people to access high-quality care, when and where they need it.
