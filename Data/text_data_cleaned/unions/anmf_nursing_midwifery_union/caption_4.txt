The ANMF has expressed serious concerns with the joint statement recently released by Ahpra on eliminating antisemitism in healthcare, in particular its adoption of the International Holocaust Remembrance Alliance (IHRA) definition of antisemitism. 
The ANMF strongly opposes antisemitism and all forms of racial or religious discrimination in the provision of healthcare and has called on the Board and Ahpra to take action, including immediately suspending the endorsement and application of the IHRA definition.
