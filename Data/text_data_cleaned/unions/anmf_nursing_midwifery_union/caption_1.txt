Recognising the collective systems of care that are working to support and strengthen breastfeeding this World Breastfeeding Week, as the ANMF works to protect, support and advocate for a positive breastfeeding culture.

Healthy, economical and sustainable - the benefits of breastfeeding are known, and its success relies on a warm chain of support and collective systems of care. 

This includes access to timely and appropriate breastfeeding support, workplace protections, and supportive social networks, rather than expecting individual mothers to carry the responsibility alone.

Let’s strengthen what works.
