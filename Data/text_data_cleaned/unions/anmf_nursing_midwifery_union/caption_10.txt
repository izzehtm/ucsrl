The ANMF will join nursing and midwifery leaders from across the country at Parliament House today for a ‘hands-on’ demonstration of the high-quality, clinical capabilities of Australia's largest health workforce.

The Parliamentary Friends of Nursing event in the Mural Hall, will highlight the growing role of nurses and midwives in delivering timely, effective health care across the community, enabling people to access high-quality care, when and where they need it.

ANMF Federal Secretary Annie Butler said the event will showcase nurse- and midwife-led models of care and the impact they can have on health outcomes when nurses and midwives work to their full scope of practice.
