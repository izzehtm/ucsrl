In June, ETU National Secretary Michael Wright joined Chief Economist at the McKell Institute, Alison Pennington, at the National Press Club in Canberra to launch Sovereign Power. 

As global supply shocks continue to put pressure on Australian industry and threaten hundreds of thousands of jobs, Australia has an opportunity to take control of its energy future. 

Sovereign Power proposes publicly owned renewable energy generation to deliver affordable, reliable power to strategic industries, strengthening our economy, protecting manufacturing, and creating secure jobs. 

Australia has done this before, and with Sovereign Power, we can do it again.
