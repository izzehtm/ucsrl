For generations, public power helped build Australia's heavy industry. Today, soaring energy costs are putting strategic industries and thousands of jobs at risk.

That's why we are calling on Labor's National Conference to back Sovereign Power, a plan for publicly owned renewable energy that delivers affordable, long-term electricity to the industries Australia depends on. This is about ending the industrial bailout cycle, protecting sovereign capability, and giving Australian manufacturing the secure energy it needs to thrive for decades to come.

The Labor Party comes from a place of democracy, and it is with these principles in mind that the ETU has been pushing for Sovereign Power at the conference. As Michael Wright said, if delegates embed Sovereign Power in the platform this year, it won’t be because the outcome was scripted. It will be because working people and their elected representatives made the case, mustered support and built momentum around the idea we need to deliver the future we want.
