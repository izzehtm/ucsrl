ETU QLD & NT members showed out to support AMWU and ETU WA Members in Port Hedland who are taking it to mining giant BHP, taking industrial action for the first time in decades. 

We rallied out the front of BHP’s Brisbane Head office to show that this blue will be fought all across Australia.

Union members at BHP Port Hedland deserve fair wages & conditions and Queensland & Northern Territory stands with you all in solidarity.

If you don’t fight. You lose! ✊⚡️
