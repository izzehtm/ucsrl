The mighty ETU was front and centre at 50th ALP National Conference, and we won Sovereign Power!
 
At the conference, the ETU was proud to make the case for Sovereign Power, a plan to secure the future of Australian heavy industry through publicly owned renewable energy. As ETU National Secretary Michael Wright told delegates, it puts Australia on a path towards a prosperous future powered by sovereign energy and built around our national interests.
 
It was great to see the conference support Sovereign Power, and vote to put Sovereign Power into the ALP National Policy Platform – the document that instructs all ALP government policy.
 
Across the conference, ETU delegates made sure the voices of electrical workers were heard loud and clear, and without the work and power of ETU members, this win wouldn’t have been possible.
