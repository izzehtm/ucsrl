The mighty ETU was in full flight at the National ALP Conference in Adelaide. We secured Sovereign Power in the party platform. This is a massive win for ETU members and a crucial point to get heavy industry powered up again - powered by publicly owned power - to keep building and making things in Australia.

ETU National Secretary Michael Wright addressed the conference, championing Sovereign Power and highlighting the need for publicly owned power in order to strengthen and to continue to build our country’s heavy manufacturing industries. 

This win is a culmination of years of hard work and advocacy by ETU members across the county, fighting for publicly owned power. 

The ETU will always fight for good, well-paid, secure, union jobs and Sovereign Power is a huge step to ensuring the energy transition puts electrical workers front and centre. 

Congratulations to all ETU members.
