Australia's heavy industry is under pressure from rising energy costs. We've seen the warning signs at Tomago, Liberty Bell Bay, Whyalla and across the manufacturing sector. Rather than relying on endless bailouts, the Commonwealth should build and own renewable energy generation and provide reliable, affordable power to industries that are critical to Australia's national interest. By delivering energy at lower cost than the private market, Sovereign Power would strengthen Australian manufacturing, protect skilled jobs, and shield our economy from global energy price shocks.
