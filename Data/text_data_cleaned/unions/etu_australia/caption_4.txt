As investment in AI infrastructure accelerates, we have a once-in-a-generation opportunity to train the next generation of electricians. The ETU is calling for stronger apprenticeship requirements on major data centre projects to ensure these developments don't just deliver technology. They should be delivering tradespeople too.

As ETU National Secretary Michael Wright said, construction demand from data centres, renewable energy projects and large infrastructure projects like the Brisbane Olympics is placing enormous pressure on the industry's workforce. Without stronger apprentice ratios, we'll deepen the skills shortage instead of solving it.

Australia needs more electricians. Every major project should be training them.
