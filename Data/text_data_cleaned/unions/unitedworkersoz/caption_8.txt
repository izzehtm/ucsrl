Since April, more than 400 workers from across Queensland have shared their experiences with United Workers Union. Their stories describe being pushed, punched, kicked, spat on, sworn at and having objects thrown at them while simply doing their jobs.

The Department's own figures show occupational violence and aggression was the leading cause of workplace injury in Queensland schools last year, with 20,833 reported incidents, including around 1,000 requiring medical treatment or hospitalisation.

Teacher Aides and School Cleaners have spoken. Minister John-Paul Langbroek must now deliver the action, staffing and support needed to make Queensland schools safer for everyone.
