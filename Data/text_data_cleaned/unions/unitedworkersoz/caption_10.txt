Day three of the United Workers Union Member Convention was about building our power. 
 
Delegates began the day hearing from First Nations members and Thomas Mayo about building power, solidarity and a stronger union.
 
Across a full day of workshops, delegates shared experiences, developed skills and worked together on how we organise, campaign and build power in our workplaces, industries and communities.
 
The ideas and discussions from workshops were brought back to the Convention floor as delegates shared what they had learned and how we can put it into action.
 
There’s one more day to go. Tomorrow, delegates will bring the work of Convention together and discuss how we take our agenda for a stronger union forward. ✊
