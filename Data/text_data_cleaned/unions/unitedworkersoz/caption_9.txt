That’s a wrap for the 2026 United Workers Union Member Convention!
 Over four days, delegates from across the country came together to share experiences, learn from each other and help shape the future direction of our union.
 Today, delegates brought the work of Convention together, reporting back, making decisions and discussing how we take our agenda forward.
 Our union is strongest when members across industries, workplaces and communities stand together.
 Delegates are heading home with new ideas, stronger connections and a shared commitment to keep organising and building power.
 Thank you to every delegate who travelled, participated, shared their experiences and helped make Convention happen.
 Now it’s time to take that energy back to our workplaces and keep building a stronger union together. ✊
