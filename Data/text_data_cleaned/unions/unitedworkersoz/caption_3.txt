The workers who make Australia's favourite biscuits have walked off the job today for the first time since the 1990s.
 
More than 160 Arnott's workers in Adelaide are taking 24 hours of protected industrial action after four years of 2.5% annual pay rises have left them 9.5% behind the cost of living.
 
As workers struggle to make ends meet, Arnott's owner KKR manages hundreds of billions of US dollars in assets globally.
 
Workers aren't asking for anything unreasonable. They're standing together for a fair deal that recognises the value of the work they do every day.
