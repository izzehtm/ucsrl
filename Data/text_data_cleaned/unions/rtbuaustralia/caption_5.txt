The Savannahlander is more than just a train. It’s a journey through Queensland’s outback, bringing visitors to remote communities and supporting local tourism.

But its future is uncertain. Damage to key sections of the track has limited its route, and without significant investment, this iconic service faces an uphill battle.

We’re standing with the workers and communities who depend on the Savannahlander. A community campaign is coming. Stay tuned to find out how you can help keep this journey alive.
