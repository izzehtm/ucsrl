Tonight across Queensland, Pacific National train drivers are arriving back to work after being locked out of their own workplace for the past 24 hours.

Locked out, not because they refused to work, but because their employer chose escalation over negotiation.

This comes after months of stalled negotiations and members overwhelmingly voting NO to the company’s offer.

Instead of returning to the table and fixing it, Pacific National escalated the dispute with one of the most aggressive industrial tactics an employer can use. 

But they’ve badly misjudged who they’re dealing with.

Train drivers are one of the most unionised group of workers in this country. With the backing of the RTBU across Australia, members are standing together for a fair agreement, safer conditions and real protections around fatigue.

These photos show it clearly. Workers turning up. Standing together. Refusing to be intimidated.

Solidarity to every Pacific National driver standing together tonight ✊
