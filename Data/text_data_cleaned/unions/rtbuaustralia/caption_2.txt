This week the RTBU released a damning report exposing the shocking state of toilets and sanitary facilities across Australia’s transport network.

Women across rail, tram and bus report filthy, unsafe or completely inadequate facilities. Some have no access to a toilet at all. It’s a system failing the basics of workplace dignity and safety.

Every worker deserves clean, safe facilities and the freedom to take a toilet break when they need one.

That’s why the RTBU is calling for a mandatory national standard for transport workers — a standard that guarantees safe, clean and accessible facilities across our industries.
