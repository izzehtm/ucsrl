Two days of national bargaining have wrapped up in Victoria for the Pacific National Intermodal Terminal Operators Enterprise Agreement.

Delegates from across the country came together with National Organiser Leanne to continue the fight for fairer conditions. Hosted by RTBU Victoria Branch, the sessions also included a visit to the Melbourne Freight Terminal to see local operations firsthand.

This is collective action in practice — a united, national effort to hold the line and win a better deal for workers.
