Solidarity with Firefighters ✊🚒

RTBU WA stands proudly with the United Firefighters Union of WA @upfuwa in their campaign for better pay and safer working conditions.

In Kalgoorlie, RTBU Organiser Cody Jensen met with local UPFU members to show our support on the ground. Meanwhile in Perth, a group of RTBU Railcar Driver Delegates showed their solidarity outside Parliament House after spotting one of the UPFU campaign fire trucks.

Frontline workers deserve respect, recognition and real change — on the tracks or the fire line.
