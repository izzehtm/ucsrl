MEAA mourns the passing of legendary music journalist, broadcaster, author and long-time MEAA member Glenn A. Baker, who joined MEAA in 1979. A towering figure in Australian music journalism, Glenn's passion, knowledge and storytelling helped shape the nation's understanding of popular music for more than five decades. From his role in the early days of double j, to his work as a broadcaster, author, record label founder and long-time Billboard editor, his influence on Australia's music culture was immeasurable.

Glenn was renowned for his encyclopaedic music knowledge, championing of Australian artists, and unwavering enthusiasm for sharing stories about music with audiences around the world.

 Our thoughts are with his family, friends, colleagues and the many musicians, journalists and music lovers whose lives he touched.
