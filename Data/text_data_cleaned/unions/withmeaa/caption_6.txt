Arts Centre Melbourne is essential to Victoria's cultural life, yet Arts Centre Melbourne workers' wages and conditions are not keeping pace with rising costs. This week, MEAA members will vote on protected industrial action as they fight for a fair agreement and call for greater government investment in the arts.
