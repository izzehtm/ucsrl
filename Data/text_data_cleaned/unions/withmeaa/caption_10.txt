Last weekend MEAA hosted 'Child's Play: developing a lifelong love for arts and culture in Australia' at the ALP National Conference in Adelaide. The event demonstrated why governments across Australia should adopt the principle of lifelong participation in the arts as a core part of cultural policy.

Arabana and Yawuru man Nathan May was joined by the Australian Youth Orchestra (AYO) String Quartet for a performance of 'Wangkarda', and supported composer, conductor and educator Annie Kwok in an interactive music session for attendees.

Former AYO CEO Colin Cornish spoke about the importance of music education, highlighting the vital role the arts play in young people's lives.
