For SDA members, a secure home is getting harder to hold onto. 

More than half rent, and 71% don't own any property at all. Whether you're renting or trying to buy, the pressure is real: 62% spent more than 30% of their income on housing last year.

Everyone deserves a safe, affordable place to live, no matter what you earn. The SDA is doing the work to make that happen, backing renters' rights and pushing for more homes.
