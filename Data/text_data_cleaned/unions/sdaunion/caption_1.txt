BREAKING: The SDA has reached a proposed settlement in its national class action against ALDI over what the SDA contended is unpaid work performed before and after rostered shifts.

Including amounts previously paid by ALDI to some ALDI workers, the total value of the outcome is expected to be more than $50 million to approximately 32,000 current and former ALDI workers, subject to final calculations and approval by the Federal Court.
