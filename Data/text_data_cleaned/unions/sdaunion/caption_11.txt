A SUPER win for young, Aussie workers 🎉 👇

Labor has announced a commitment to remove the 30 hour threshold so all working teenagers can receive super, regardless of the hours they’ve worked 👏😍

I, alongside the @sdaunion , have been advocating for this change for some time now and it’s such heartening news for young Aussie workers that work so hard for every dollar they earn 😍

The outdated under-18, 30-hour rule is on the way out 🚀

Labor is stepping up to support young people early in their working life so they can retire more comfortably at the other end ❤️
