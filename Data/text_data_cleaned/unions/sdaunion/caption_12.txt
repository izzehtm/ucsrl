Today at the Labor Engaged Fringe during the ALP National Conference, the John Curtin Research Centre was proud to host the launch panel for Defending Australia: A National Artificial Intelligence Sovereign Capability and Security Agenda for the 21st Century.

A big thank you to The Hon. Chris Picton MP, Gerard Dwyer and Dr Dominic Meagher for an insightful discussion about one of the defining public policy challenges of our time.

Our conversation, moderated by our ED Nick @dyrenfurth, ranged from sovereign AI capability and national security to industrial strategy, critical infrastructure, democratic resilience and the future of work. A common theme emerged: artificial intelligence is no longer simply a technology issue. It is a question of economic sovereignty, national capability and ensuring Australia has the skills, institutions and industrial base to shape—not simply consume—the technologies that will define the decades ahead.

It was particularly fitting to hold this discussion in Adelaide, home to the world-leading Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML) and at the heart of Australia's growing defence, advanced manufacturing and innovation ecosystem.

Thank you to everyone who joined us and contributed to a lively audience discussion.

The conversation continues on Saturday morning at 8.00am, when we launch our latest report, Manufacturing Homes: A National Strategy for Solving Australia's Housing Crisis, with The Hon. Clare O'Neil MP, followed by a panel featuring Lachlan Kerwood-McCall, Tim Woods and Michael O'Connor. We look forward to seeing you there.
