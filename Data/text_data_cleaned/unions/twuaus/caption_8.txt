TWU members have voted unanimously to take protected industrial action at Cleanaway C&I Dandenong! 

Waste workers at Dandenong have been handed an insulting offer on pay and conditions, and they’ve made it clear: enough is enough.

This dispute comes against the backdrop of Cleanaway’s shocking safety record. Cleanaway workers around the country are pushing for safe conditions, with pressure mounting on workers to work longer and faster while pay goes backwards.

Last week ALP National Conference made a resolution to move companies like Cleanaway out of the federal Comcare insurance system, into the better-equipped state systems for better oversight and accountability for safety.

We are sending a message to Cleanaway, if they refuse to improve these appalling conditions, we will force them to.
