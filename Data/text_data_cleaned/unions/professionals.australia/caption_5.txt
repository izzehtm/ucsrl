ACT Government Scientists just made history, walking off the job in their first ever strike and marching on the ACT Legislative Assembly for fair hazard pay. 

After a decade of real wage cuts and months of government silence, they’re done being told there’s money for everything except their safety. They enter meth labs, handle fentanyl and other toxic chemicals, and keep the community safe.

All without the allowance already paid to comparable frontline roles. 

The turnout was huge and the message  @was clear: recognise the risks, put a fair offer on the table, or expect more action from united workers who won’t back down.
