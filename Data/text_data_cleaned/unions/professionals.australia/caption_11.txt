The Australian Energy Market Operator (AEMO) runs the systems that keep electricity and gas flowing to millions of homes and businesses. The engineers, analysts and specialists who do that work have just sent their employer a clear message.

For the second time, AEMO employees have voted down the proposed enterprise agreement.

Two offers. Two rejections. One message management can't miss: this workforce is live, and it will not be pressured into accepting less than it's worth.

When professionals stand together, they control the switch. Workers held the line, staying united, to reject management's offer.

This is what collective power looks like!
