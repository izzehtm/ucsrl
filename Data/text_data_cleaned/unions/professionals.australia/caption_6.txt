Michele O'Neil and Sally McManus are leaving the ACTU this year after 8 years running Australia's union movement together, the first women to hold both top jobs at the same time in its 99 year history.
 
A lot changed under their leadership.
Wages moved in sectors that had been stuck for years, the gender pay gap fell to its lowest point on record, and membership, which had been going backwards for decades, started climbing again.
 
They've said the next generation of leaders needs to be ready for what's coming, including AI's impact on jobs. That's already a live issue for our members, and one we'll keep fighting on long after this handover.
 
Professionals Australia is an ACTU affiliate. We admire the 8 years Michele and Sally spent standing up for working people, and we want to thank them for it.
