We want you for an HSR at Snowy Hydro 2.0!

To nominate, email new lead organiser Jonathan Farry at jfarry@pa.org.au

A Health and Safety Representative is elected by their workmates to speak for them on safety. HSRs get trained. They must be consulted before changes that affect health and safety. They can inspect the site, investigate hazards, issue provisional improvement notices and direct unsafe work to stop.

Strong HSRs can turn a site around. That is exactly why Webuild fought so hard to keep white collar workers out of the process, refusing to negotiate work groups for engineers, supervisors, surveyors and technical staff. We took it to SafeWork NSW. The regulator determined separate work groups across Polo Flat, Tantangara, Lobs Hole and Marica.

We won the right. Now let’s use it! #snowyhydro #safetyfirst #HSR #nsw #ausunions
