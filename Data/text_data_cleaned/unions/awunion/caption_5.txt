On Day 1 of the 50th ALP National Conference, our National Secretary Paul Farrow backed Labor's industrial plan to build an economy that serves working people. 

Our gas belongs to us, but instead is being used to supercharge the profits of multinational gas exporters. That's why the AWU is calling on the Labor government to finalise a gas reservation that is fair for workers and the industries that power the nation.

Australian gas must benefit Australian people and Australian industry!
