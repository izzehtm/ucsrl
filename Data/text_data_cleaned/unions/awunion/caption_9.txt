The federal government is currently working through the nuts and bolts of its coming gas reservation, and the AWU has a strong message for gas exporters.

The only way a reservation works is if it really does apply to the big players and treats everyone equally. Any framework that doles out unfair carve-outs will fail. One set of rules needs to apply firmly and its needs to apply to all.

If you want to extract Australia's gas, you must set aside a portion for Australians. No dodgy leave passes, no exceptions!
