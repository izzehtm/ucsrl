Federal Labor has announced a feasibility study into building a new fuel refinery in WA – a first step towards restoring Australia’s lost refining capacity.
 
They must back up this announcement with a long-term commitment to supporting new investment and job creation in the industry. We need to boost local fuel holdings and refining to meet the moment, while also realising our huge potential in alternative fuels in the longer term.
 
The government has done well to secure emergency fuel import deals in the wake of the Iran-Israel-US war. But relying on a 'friends and allies' fuel security model is a wager, not a strategy.
