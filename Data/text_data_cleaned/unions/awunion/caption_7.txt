AWU WA Branch Secretary Brad Gandy and VIC Branch Secretary Ronnie Hayden travelled to Wales this week representing the Australian Workers’ Union at Community’s 2026 Biennial Delegate Conference.

The conference theme - Strong Union, Strong Future - could not be more relevant.

Across industries and across borders, workers are facing many of the same challenges of rising living costs, insecure work, changing industries, new technology, and overall pressure on wages and conditions.

A key message from the conference is that unions must continue more than ever to organise, grow and empower members at the workplace.

The conference heard first hand from members and delegates regarding the importance of building a strong union by standing together, speaking up and organising on the ground.

Brad and Ronnie met with union leaders, delegates and international representatives to share ideas covering campaigning, workplace organising, leadership and the next generation of union activists.

While our unions may operate in different countries our purpose is the same, giving working people a strong collective voice and delivering better wages, safer workplaces, secure jobs and respect at work.

The lesson from the conference is clear:

When workers are organised, informed and united, we have the power to shape that future, rather than simply having it imposed on us.

Strong unions. Strong workplaces. Strong communities. Strong future.
