On this day in 1907, the AWU made history by securing Australia's first-ever Federal Industrial Award! 

The Pastoral Workers’ Award increased wages by 20% and set 48-hour working weeks for shearers across the country, with work to stop at midday Saturday. 

The AWU President at the time, William Spence wrote in The Worker: “It only remains now for the members of The Australian Workers’ Union to remain active and true to their principles... to secure still more improvement in many other directions.” 

It stands true to this day. We now cover workers in 78 Industrial Awards across Australia, securing better wages and conditions, improved safety standards, and secure jobs for our members!
