Happy birthday to prime minister and proud AWU member Gough Whitlam! 🎂

Gough was prime minister of Australia between 1972 and 1975. The brief but revolutionary Whitlam Government secured universal healthcare, free university education, and equal pay, built nation-building freeway and sewerage programs, and abolished the White Australia policy. 

Gough was also a staunch advocate for workers and proud long-term member of the AWU NSW Branch. He passed away in 2014, at the age of 98.
