On Day 2 of the 50th ALP National Conference, AWU Assistant National Secretary Chris Donovan backed reforms to improve jobs, skills, and opportunities for Australians. 

While the Federal Labor government passed Same Job Same Pay laws, employers are using a new loophole to pay some workers less, by misclassifying them as 'trainees'.

That's why we're supporting an amendment to make it absolutely clear to employers - two workers doing the same job, at the same site, must be paid the same.
