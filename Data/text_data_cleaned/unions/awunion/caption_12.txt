Payday Super is officially here! 🎉

From now on, your super contributions must be paid into your nominated super fund at the same time as your salary or wages. 

This means that workers will see more gains from their super, as you'll start accumulating interest on your payments faster! 

Make sure you check your super fund seven business days after your wages come in, to make sure you're getting paid - don't rely on payslips alone.
