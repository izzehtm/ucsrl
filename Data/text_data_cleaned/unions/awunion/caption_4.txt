An incredible 140 years stronger together. 

The AWU recently celebrated our 140th birthday at original site of the Eureka Stockade in Ballarat, a place where our mighty union has gathered to mark 50, 100, and 120 years. 

From the shearing sheds of 1886 to industries across Australia today, our fight remains the same - better wages and conditions, safer workplaces, and secure jobs. 

Thank you to every member from every generation who has kept this union strong.
