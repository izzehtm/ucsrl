We’re thrilled by this outcome for our member Karlene Chandler. The Commission’s decision is significant and paves the way for workers who have caring responsibilities just like Ms Chandler to secure work from home rights.

@westpac not only failed to meet its obligations to consider Ms Chandler’s request to work from home in accordance with the Fair Work Act, but the grounds on which Westpac refused her request were found not to be based on reasonable business grounds.

Our members highly value their flexible work arrangements and have organised their lives around it. This is a fantastic win that demonstrates employers need to take flexible working seriously and genuinely consider workers’ situation, if they want to retain talented staff in the industry.
