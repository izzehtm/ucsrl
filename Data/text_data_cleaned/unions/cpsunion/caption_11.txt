CPSU members and delegates would like to thank ACTU leaders Sally McManus and Michele O’Neil for their tireless efforts leading the Australian union movement over much of the past decade. 

They were the first all-female leadership team in the ACTU’s 99-year history. Under their direction, union membership is growing and wages are increasing. 

They also successfully campaigned for the right to disconnect law, same job, same pay protections, wage theft criminalisation and ensuring minimum pay, insurance and safety standards for gig economy workers. 

Both Sally and Michele were great friends and allies of the CPSU lending their support to many of our campaigns including Working From Home, outsourcing, opposing public sector job cuts, CSL bargaining and protecting the CSIRO. 

And while there are many challenges ahead for working Australians, these two unionists are leaving the movement in a strong and united position. 

CPSU members congratulate them of their many achievements and wish them well for their future endeavours.
