Do you think it's fair? 

Private labour hire companies hold contracts in the hundreds of millions in Australia.

Workers hired through these companies are doing ATO work, directed by the ATO, on ATO systems - but are paid 40% less than directly employed ATO staff.

And in worse working conditions to boot.

That's why the CPSU have made a submission to the landmark 'Same Job Same Pay' case. This arrangement is unfair for the labour hire workers, the ATO workers, and the taxpayer.
