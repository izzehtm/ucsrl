Be part of organising Reclaim the Night 2026.

Reclaim the Night is a global movement calling for an end to gendered violence, harassment and victim-blaming, while working towards safer and more just communities.

Whether you're a worker, student, activist or simply passionate about creating safer communities, there are many ways to get involved.
