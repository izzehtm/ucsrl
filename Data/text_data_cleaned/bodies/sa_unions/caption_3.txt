A huge thank you to Sally McManus and Michele O’Neil following the announcement that they will step down as Secretary and President of the Australian Council of Trade Unions.

Sally and Michele have been remarkable leaders of the trade union movement. Their contributions across almost a decade of leadership will be felt by Australian workers and their families for generations to come.

Under Sally and Michele’s leadership, the union movement successfully fought off attempts by the Turnbull and Morrison governments to reduce and undermine the rights of Australian workers.

Since then, with Sally and Michele at the helm workers have benefitted from dozens of new workplace rights which have raised wages, reduced the gender pay gap, delivered more secure jobs, and made workplaces safer.

For the South Australian union movement, Sally and Michele have been more than national leaders - they have been steadfast allies and comrades to South Australian workers. 

We thank Sally and Michele for their tireless work on behalf of working people and wish them both the very best for the future.
