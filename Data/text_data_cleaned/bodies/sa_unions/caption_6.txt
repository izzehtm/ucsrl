Congratulations to @abcadelaide's Sophie Holder for winning the Max Fatchen Award for Best Young Journalist at the 2026 SA Media Awards!

SA Unions is proud to sponsor this award because young journalists like Sophie are the future of an ethical, independent, and strong media here in South Australia.

The SA Media Awards are made possible by @withmeaa, the union for media professionals which works tirelessly to support journalists and safeguard the future of the industry.

Congratulations again to Sophie on her well-deserved win!
