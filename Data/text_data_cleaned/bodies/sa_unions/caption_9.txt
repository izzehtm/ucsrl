Join union members at the NAIDOC March this Friday 10 July as we celebrate fifty years of NAIDOC Week.

For five decades, NAIDOC Week has celebrated First Nations culture, called for truth, and honoured resistance. This year’s theme - Fifty Years of Deadly - is a tribute to the people who built this movement and a commitment to continue the work.

We’re kicking off at 10:30am at Tarntanyangga (Victoria Square) and marching up King William St to Parliament House.

Stick around at Tarntanyangga after the march to enjoy music, food, market stalls, and activities for the kids at the NAIDOC Family Festival until 3:30pm.

Come along to stand in solidarity with Elders who have led the way and young people who are carrying on the fight for justice with new tools and the same fire.
