There’s still time to apply to be part of Organising Works in 2027 📢

Organising Works is a national training and leadership development program run by the Australian Council of Trade Unions. 

Across a 12-month paid traineeship, you’ll learn how to build worker power and win alongside members through a combination of education and real-world organising experience. 

The course is designed for anyone committed to social change and collective action and is a great opportunity for delegates, HSRs, and union activists ✊

Applications close this Sunday 12 July.
