When someone gets hurt doing their job, they deserve support to recover, stay connected to work, and avoid financial hardship.

Too many workers dealing with South Australia’s compensation system face delays, poor communication, and unfair barriers which stop them receiving the support they need.

An upcoming independent review of the Return To Work system is a major opportunity to re-build our compensation system.

People with direct experience of the system know where it’s failing and what needs to change.

That’s why we need to hear your story – to fight for a system that treats injured workers as people, not just claim numbers.

Share your experiences through our online survey and help us create a compensation system that delivers dignity, fairness, and respect.
