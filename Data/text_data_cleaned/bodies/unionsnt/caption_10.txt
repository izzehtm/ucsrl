🎡 Heading to the Darwin Show? Come and say hello to the MigraNT Workers' Hub!

Whether you're working in hospitality, agriculture, retail, cleaning, construction, or any other industry, your rights at work matter. If you're a migrant worker on a temporary visa in the Northern Territory, you don't have to face workplace issues alone. We're here to help.

📍 Find us at the Darwin Show
🗓️ 23–25 July

Drop by our stall, have a chat with our friendly team, ask questions, and pick up information about your workplace rights. No matter your visa or your job, everyone deserves to be treated fairly and with respect.
