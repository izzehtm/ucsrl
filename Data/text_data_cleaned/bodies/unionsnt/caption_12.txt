The Australian Nursing and Midwifery Federation Northern Territory (ANMF NT) is calling for urgent action to eliminate workplace violence and aggression against nurses and midwives, launching its Stop Workplace Violence and Aggression campaign on 20 July 2026.

Every day, nurses and midwives across the Northern Territory face verbal abuse, threats, intimidation and physical assault while caring for the community. ANMF NT says violence should never be accepted as "part of the job."

Recent ANMF NT member surveys highlight the scale of the problem:

· 57% of respondents experienced workplace violence or aggression, with 9% of respondents facing these events daily.
· Only half of the respondents feel safe at work
· 40% see the same violent behaviours occur repeatedly in the same workplaces.
· 37% report delayed or inadequate responses.
· Of the 40% of nurses and midwives who reported their incidents, almost 60% said nothing happens afterwards.

More results from the ANMF NT members survey will be released on July 20 at the campaign launch.
