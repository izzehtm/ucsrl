SUPER IS YOUR MONEY. UNION MEMBERS JUST MADE IT EASIER TO  GET EVERY CENT OF SUPER YOUR ENTITLED TO.

From tomorrow, your employer must pay your superannuation contribution on pay day. Not months later - if at all.

The truth is, Australian workers were losing $4.7 billion  in unpaid super every year. $4.7 billion every single year taken out of the pocket of Australian workers.

A big part of the problem was not being able to track what super contributions had been paid in relation to your wages.

So union members campaigned for pay day super. All your super contributions must now be paid at the same time as your wages. So any disparity can be seen clearly and fixed.

It's one more way Unions are making sure every single Australian worker gets their fair share.
