Yesterday the community gathered to celebrate VicHealth, braving the wet Melbourne winter in bright colours.

For nearly 40 years VicHealth has provided independent and innovative health promotion programs that put the health of Victorians first. VicHealth is a vital public service.

We know that without proper investment in health prevention, life expectancy decreases and ambulance and hospital demand skyrockets.

Victoria needs VicHealth to stay independent, fully-funded and strong.
