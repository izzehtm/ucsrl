Thomas Costa, assistant secretary of Unions NSW, slams One Nation for being anti-worker in an explosive speech at the ALP state conference. 

“One Nation have never lifted a finger for a workers’ bargaining power. They’ve never won a pay rise. They’ve never closed a loophole. They’ve never backed a single measure to stop wage theft.”
 
The speech comes as the NSW union movement ramps up the fight for a Labour Hire Licensing scheme to protect workers from scam companies and dodgy employers that have already been banned in other states. Join the fight at the link in our bio and sign the petition to BAN SCAM COMPANIES FROM NSW.
