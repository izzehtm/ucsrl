Teachers deserve a safe workplace, just like any other worker.

They also deserve a government and leadership that doesn’t brush off an increase in assaults and violence as a result of an encouragement to report.

As the Australian Education Union Tasmania points out, violence in our schools is being driven by socio-economic complexity and school resourcing failing to keep pace with student demand. 

We need action to keep school staff safe alongside a government willing to tackle the underlying issues facing students at home.
