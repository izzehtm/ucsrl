Unions Tasmania would like to extend our deepest condolences and solidarity to the Cocker family on the passing of Simon. 

Simon was a dedicated trade unionist and led Unions Tasmania as Secretary between 2004 and 2017. Prior to this, he was the Regional Secretary of the CPSU - Community and Public Sector Union's Tasmanian branch. 

Simon led Unions Tasmania during the successful Your Rights at Work campaign against John Howard's WorkChoices laws. The national union movement mounted one of its fiercest campaigns in history. Labor won all five Tasmanian federal seats as the country rejected the Liberal Party's attack on worker's pay and conditions. 

Simon was a passionate workplace safety advocate and worked tirelessly for action on safe asbestos removal from workplaces and the broader community. 

You would also see Simon as an enthusiastic member of the The Tasmanian Grassroots Union Choir, sharing his passion for working class story telling through music.

He will be deeply missed. Vale, Simon Cocker.
