Today our community in the ACT gathered at the National Workers Memorial to honour those who have lost their lives while on the job. We reflected on the fundamental right to safety at work - especially the often unrecognised psychosocial hazards workers face. Workplace safety must never be delayed, ignored, or dismissed. We acknowledge and sincerely thank all those who attended in particular, Anne Maree, Kay, Barney, Professor Talaulikar and Commissioner Agius for their courage, words, and advocacy. We remember those we have lost and fight like hell for the living.
