Two years ago, Queensland led the way by introducing paid Reproductive Health Leave for many public sector workers. Now, we want to know how it's working in practice. 

If you currently have access to Reproductive Health Leave, we want to hear from you. Whether you've used the entitlement or not, your experience will help build the evidence we need to campaign for something we believe should be available to every worker in Australia. 

Our anonymous survey only takes a few minutes to complete, but your voice will help shape the future of workplace rights, improve support for workers, and strengthen the case for expanding this important entitlement nationwide. 

Because reproductive health affects all of us, in different ways, at different times.
